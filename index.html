YT Automation Panel

A personal automation tool that helps me publish short-form and long-form videos to my own YouTube channels and keep an eye on how they perform. It is a private utility I assembled for my own account, and it is not a public service — there is nothing here for visitors to use or sign up for.

What it does

  • Uploads videos that I prepare (downloaded from third-party platforms together with minor editing such as trimming) to my own YouTube channels, typically on a daily schedule.
  • Reads public statistics of my channels — view counts, likes, watch time and similar metrics — to generate reports for myself.
  • Stores everything on my own server. The panel is only reachable via my local tunnel and is not exposed to the public internet.
